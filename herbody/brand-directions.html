Four ways to be the complete scoop.

Same product, same wedge, same legal guardrails — four different psychological bets on who she is when she buys it. Each direction is a complete identity: palette, type, wordmark, pouch, voice, tagline, photography, social feel and Amazon hero. The line-up on white and the scored matrix at the end are there so you choose with the same eyes Amazon gives her: two seconds, one thumbnail.

Recommendation

Launch on 01 The Editor's system with 02 Berry Monolith's appetite. Keep the approved alabaster-and-black-cherry pouch (01) and borrow 02's photography, pour videos and sensory copy so the brand never goes cold. Hold 03's expert voice as the content pillar for the core buyer — it's a voice, not a redesign. Bank 04 for run two, when TikTok Shop and a second flavour are affordable. The one live test worth running: the flipped berry-face pouch (02) as a thumbnail A/B in the first PPC campaign — if it lifts click-through by a clear margin, you switch the pack at reorder without changing the brand.

If you'd rather choose than blend — three questions

  • Who do you picture holding it? A 44-year-old who lifts twice a week → 01 or 03. A 27-year-old with a ring light → 02 or 04.
  • What's your first channel really going to be? Amazon search → 01/02/03. TikTok Shop → 04.
  • What do you want strangers to say about the pouch? "Looks serious" → 01. "Looks delicious" → 02. "Looks grown-up" → 03. "Looks fun" → 04.

Shared rules

What stays fixed whichever way you go

Name architecture

herbody is the house; BODY BEAUTY is the product; "the complete daily" is the descriptor that carries the wedge. Future SKUs take the same two-word shape (e.g. Body Calm, Body Bright) — never "Her-" prefixes or "-Glow" suffixes.

The spec strip

Four cells, always, in this order: 5g creatine · 5g collagen · [24] vitamins & minerals · myo-inositol. It is the brand's proof object across pack, listing, social and site. Confirm the vitamin count against the Black Label spec.

Claims

Only GB-register wording: biotin/zinc for hair and skin, vitamin C for collagen formation, B-vitamins for tiredness and fatigue, creatine's performance claim at ≥3g. Never: diuretic, bloat, recomposition, enhancer, tone, hormone balance, menopause relief, clinically proven.

The enemy

The £59 shelf of half-finished tubs and the sprinkle-dose "for her" products. Every direction attacks the same enemy; only the tone changes.

Proof furniture

Numbers on the front, one third-party mark when earned, review count on the listing as soon as it's respectable. Adjectives are not proof.

The ritual

"Your daily scoop" — the phrase, the copper scoop, the 7am image. Ritual is the moat; every direction names it.

Pick the driver, and the rest of the brand follows.
