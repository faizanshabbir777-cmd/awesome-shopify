herbody on TikTok Shop

A detailed plan for launching BODY BEAUTY on TikTok Shop UK with 500 of the 1,300 first-run units, alongside the Amazon UK launch. Built for a founder with a full-time job who will not be on camera daily: creators make the content, TikTok's automation buys the traffic, and your job is briefing, approving and reading the numbers.

The shape of it
Split the units, but not the launch. Amazon goes live first; TikTok Shop follows two weeks later so every brand search a creator video generates lands on a listing with reviews. TikTok is run as an affiliate-first machine, not a founder-led one.
  • 01Supplements are a restricted category on TikTok Shop UK. Qualification takes 2–4 weeks and gates everything, so it starts now.
  • 02Of 500 units, 130 are marketing inventory (creator samples, LIVE hosts, replacements). 370 are for sale.
  • 03Fulfilment goes through Fulfilled by TikTok. The 72-hour ship SLA is not compatible with a day job.
  • 04Paid traffic is one Product GMV Max campaign, switched on only once 10 or more creator videos exist.
  • 05Every creator gets a one-page claims sheet. On this platform a single "bloating" or "hormones" video can pull the listing.

The seeding allocation is deliberately heavy. On TikTok Shop the content is the marketing, and the industry planning ratio is that 40–60% of seeded creators post, with the best programmes averaging more than one video per sample. 100 samples should produce 45–70 videos. That library is what GMV Max needs to work and what the listing needs to convert, and it is far cheaper per video than any agency.

Should you split at all?

Yes, with one condition. Amazon UK is where the category is bought; TikTok is where it is discovered, and TikTok discovery spills into Amazon brand searches within hours of a video moving. Running both means the spill-over is captured. The condition is sequencing: if TikTok goes first, those brand searches hit an Amazon listing with no reviews and the spill-over is wasted. So Amazon goes live, Vine is enrolled, and TikTok launches roughly two weeks later. The 800 Amazon units include the 30 for Vine. Hold nothing back as a float; if TikTok outsells Amazon, transfer from FBA stock in week 5.

Weeks are relative to TikTok Shop launch day (L). Amazon goes live at week −2. The new-seller 3% commission window runs for 30 days from onboarding completion, so complete onboarding no earlier than week −1 to keep it covering launch.

Weeks −6 to −4

Phase 0 · Get allowed to sell

Supplements are a restricted category on TikTok Shop UK. Nothing else in this plan matters until the category qualification is granted, and the review takes two to four weeks. Weight-loss, detox, hormone and sexual-enhancement supplements are prohibited outright; general vitamins, minerals, protein, collagen and creatine are permitted with qualification.

  • Day 1

    Open the seller account as HerBody Ltd at seller-uk.tiktok.com with Companies House number, director ID, UK bank account and the registered address. Do not complete every onboarding step yet; the 3% new-seller commission clock starts when onboarding is marked complete.

  • Day 1–3

    Apply for the Health & Supplements category. Build one document pack and reuse it for Amazon: FBO registration confirmation from your local authority, Black Label's GMP certificate, a batch COA (third-party if you have it), the final label artwork PDF, product liability insurance certificate, and the company's UK address. Where TikTok asks for a "product qualification", upload the label and COA.

  • Day 2

    Apply for Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT). FBT's UK rate card charges around £0.78 per item for parcels up to 0.5kg plus small daily storage; the pouch at 384g plus a mailer sits inside that tier. Seller shipping is £0 on orders under £22 and £1.99 at £22 and over. With FBT, the 72-hour ship SLA is TikTok's problem, not yours.

  • Week −6

    Decide the price once, for both channels. TikTok buyers compare against Amazon in the same minute; keep list price identical and let TikTok's vouchers do the discounting. The calculator below uses £34.99 as the working assumption; change it there.

  • Week −5

    Write the creator claims sheet (below) and have it checked by the same food-law consultant doing the label review. This is the document that protects the listing.

  • Week −5

    Register on the Affiliate Centre and set the Open Plan at 15% commission. Health and beauty are the categories where creators expect the top of the range; below 12% the good ones do not apply.

Weeks −4 to −2

Phase 1 · Build the listing and seed the creators

  • Week −4

    Shoot five listing videos (9:16, 15–30 seconds, no faces needed): the scoop going into water and dissolving; the "one scoop replaces this shelf" flat-lay; the back-panel spec strip read out on screen; a taste-test reaction from a friend; the pouch's tear strip and first open. A phone, a window and a plain surface. These become the listing's video carousel and the first ten GMV Max creatives.

  • Week −4

    Write the listing. Title: "herbody BODY BEAUTY · Creatine 5g + Collagen 5g + Multivitamin + Myo-Inositol · Strawberry Raspberry · 30 Scoops". Bullets in this order: what is in one scoop with doses; the authorised claims verbatim (creatine and physical performance at 3g/day, vitamin C and collagen formation, biotin and zinc for skin, hair and nails, magnesium for tiredness); flavour and mixability; how to take it; who makes it and where. No "bloating", "hormones", "cycle", "PCOS", "menopause", "recomposition" or "glow-up" anywhere, including hashtags.

  • Week −4

    Ship 130 units to FBT as soon as the pouches land (100 for seeding is sent from home, below). Book the inbound; FBT receiving takes 3–7 days.

  • Week −4 to −2

    Seeding wave 1: 100 target invitations. In the Affiliate Centre, filter creators by UK, women, health/fitness/beauty, 5,000–80,000 followers, GMV in the last 30 days above zero, and average video views above 5,000. Send 200 target invitations at 20% commission with a free sample to get 100 acceptances (expect 40–55% acceptance). Post the samples yourself with a printed claims sheet and a handwritten line in each. Three tranches so content arrives in a stream, not a lump.

  • Week −4

    Reserve 20 of the 100 for the community IKSIR was built for: UK Muslim women creators in fitness and modest-fashion niches. This is where the halal ingredient statement, the charity pledge and your own story carry weight, and TikTok is the only channel where they do. Same brand, same pouch, a different set of hands.

  • Week −2

    Amazon listing goes live and Vine is enrolled. From here every TikTok video that moves has somewhere to send the brand search.

Weeks −2 to launch

Phase 2 · Content arrives, machine gets built

  • Daily, 20 min

    Review incoming creator videos in the Affiliate Centre. Every video is checked against the claims sheet before you do anything with it. A non-compliant video gets a polite message and a request to re-cut; do not add it to any ad campaign. Compliant videos get the ad authorisation code requested (Spark Ads permission) so GMV Max can use them.

  • Week −1

    Complete onboarding to start the 30-day 3% commission window. Set up two vouchers: a shop-wide 10% welcome voucher for new followers, and a £3 off voucher that creators can attach to their videos. Enrol in co-funded promotions when the Shop Performance Score allows (2.5 minimum; new shops get a free-trial period where TikTok funds 100%).

  • Week −1

    Build the Product GMV Max campaign in Ads Manager but leave it paused: daily budget £40, ROI target set at TikTok's recommendation, all authorised creator videos and your five listing videos attached. It needs a minimum of ten creatives and a seven-day untouched run to be eligible for ROI protection.

  • Week −1

    Post to the brand account three times: a pouch reveal, a creator video reposted with permission, the "what is in one scoop" spec strip. The brand account exists so the listing has a home, not to be a channel.

Launch week

Phase 3 · Day by day

  • Mon · L

    Listing live with all five videos and the first 15–25 creator videos linked. Welcome voucher on. Message every creator who has posted: "we are live, your link is active, the £3 voucher is attached to your video". Raise commission on the Open Plan to 18% for the first 14 days.

  • Tue · L+1

    Turn on GMV Max at £40/day. Do not touch it for seven days: no pauses, no budget changes over 50%, no ROI target edits. Add new creator videos to it as they come in.

  • Wed · L+2

    First check of the numbers: video views per creator, click-through to the product card, and the product card's conversion rate. Benchmarks: 1–3% conversion from video is healthy, 8–12% from LIVE.

  • Thu · L+3

    Send 20 wave-2 target invitations to whoever has converted at all in wave 1, at 22% commission, plus a second sample for a "two weeks in" video. Book two hosted LIVEs for week +2 with creators who already sell in LIVE, at 20% commission plus a £150 host fee each. You are not the host.

  • Fri · L+4

    Reply to every product question and review. Questions on a TikTok listing are public and copied into future videos; answers must stay inside the claims sheet.

  • Sat–Sun

    Nothing. FBT ships, creators post, GMV Max runs. Check the Seller Centre once for any listing violation notice; those need a response within the stated window.

Weeks +1 to +8

Phase 4 · Run it and decide

  • Weekly, 1 hr

    The Sunday review: units sold by source (organic creator, GMV Max, LIVE, brand account), cost per unit sold, commission paid, ad spend, refund rate, and stock left in FBT. One spreadsheet, one row per week.

  • Week +2

    Two hosted LIVEs. A LIVE with a flash deal (£5 off for 60 minutes) and a creator who already runs supplement LIVEs is the highest-conversion format on the platform. Attach LIVE GMV Max at £30 for each session.

  • Week +3

    Scale what works. If GMV Max is at or above the ROI target, raise the daily budget by 30–50% at most once a week. If it is below, add creatives before adding money; the fix for GMV Max is almost always more videos, not more budget.

  • Week +4

    Commission back to 15% on the Open Plan; keep the top ten creators on 20% targeted plans. Send each of them a "month two" sample so the repeat story ("I've finished a pouch") gets told.

  • Week +5

    Stock check. If fewer than 120 units remain in FBT, transfer 100 from FBA. If more than 250 remain, move £ from GMV Max into a fourth LIVE and a 15% voucher week.

  • Week +6

    Reorder decision with Black Label. The trigger is sell-through, not sentiment: 370 units gone or a clear path to gone by week +8 at a positive contribution per unit means reorder at the next price break.

The creator claims sheet

This goes in every sample parcel and every target invitation message. It is the single most important document in the plan. TikTok removes listings for creator claims, the ASA ruled against Vivo Life in January 2025 for a creator's B12 video, and a food supplement that is marketed for weight loss is prohibited on the platform outright.

herbody · what you can say on camera

Say what it is: "one scoop, every day: 5g creatine, 5g collagen, a full multivitamin and myo-inositol, strawberry raspberry". Say what you noticed: taste, mixability, how easy it is, that it replaced the four things you were buying. Say these, word for word, if you want a benefit: "creatine increases physical performance in successive bursts of short-term, high-intensity exercise"; "vitamin C contributes to normal collagen formation for the normal function of skin"; "biotin and zinc contribute to the maintenance of normal skin, hair and nails"; "magnesium contributes to a reduction of tiredness and fatigue".

Fine

  • "5g is the dose used in the studies; most women's creatines are 3g"
  • "I take it in the morning with water" (your routine)
  • "One scoop replaced my creatine, collagen and multivitamin"
  • "Made in the UK by a GMP-certified manufacturer"
  • Taste, texture, the scoop, the pouch, the price per day
  • "No porcine ingredients" (factual, once confirmed by the manufacturer)
  • Say "commission paid" or #ad; TikTok labels affiliate videos but say it anyway

Never

  • Weight loss, fat loss, "lean", "recomposition", "toned", "bloating", "debloat"
  • Hormones, cycle, PMS, PCOS, menopause, fertility, "balance"
  • Anti-ageing, wrinkles, "glow-up", "plumper skin", hair growth
  • Energy, focus, mood, brain, sleep, anxiety
  • Before-and-after photos or "results in X weeks"
  • "Clinically proven", "doctors recommend", any disease or symptom
  • Comparisons naming another brand

What TikTok changes about the brand

Nothing on the pouch. Three things around it.

  • The founder story becomes visible, in text. One pinned brand video and the listing's "about" panel can say who makes this and why, in the past tense and with specifics. Creators repeat what they are given, so give them two sentences worth repeating.
  • The charity mechanic works here where it was overhead on Amazon. Keep the Charities Act in mind: "we donate £1 per pouch to [named charity]" in advertising makes HerBody Ltd a commercial participator and needs a written agreement. The simple legal version is the one in the launch plan: donate quarterly, say so afterwards with the receipt. Creators can repeat a past-tense fact.
  • The 20 community creators are the IKSIR insight, delivered. If that tranche outperforms the other 80 on conversion, that is the evidence for a later IKSIR edition, and you will have it by week +4.

One risk to name. TikTok Shop UK's supplement enforcement is stricter than Amazon's and is applied to creator speech you do not control. The plan handles it with the claims sheet, the pre-authorisation check and a re-brief on any violation. It cannot make the risk zero. If the listing is pulled, the 370 units are still sellable on Amazon and the creator videos still drive brand searches there, which is a second reason Amazon goes first.
